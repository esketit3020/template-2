# Template 2 — Australian Roofing Website

Three selectable site-wide design concepts for Australian roofing businesses, based on `template-1` without storm damage or insurance-claim positioning.

- **Trade Bold:** charcoal and burnt orange.
- **Coastal Clean:** navy and ocean blue.
- **Architectural:** forest and warm stone.

Use the Horizon Digital style switcher at the top of the site to preview each variant. The design affects the entire site while the service pages, suburb pages, local SEO structure and quote form share one codebase.

See [TEMPLATE2.md](TEMPLATE2.md) for setup and handover notes. The Ironbark business identity, licence, reviews and project details are source-template examples and must be replaced with verified client information before publishing.
